That One Question

You once asked me: “How do I complete you?” ❤️

You don't complete me because I'm incomplete without you. You complete the parts of my life that were always meant to be shared.

You made ordinary things feel worth telling someone about. You made me more expressive, more thoughtful and more aware of another person's feelings.

You don't become my missing half. You make the life I already have feel fuller because you're in it.

Before you get busy… 💜
